## Business Capability Pipeline

```
NetBox (Expected State)
    ↓
InventoryService.synchronize()
    ↓
Persisted NetBox Snapshot
    ↓
Real Network Discovery (via collectors)
    ↓
Persisted Live Snapshot
    ↓
ComparisonEngine (6 comparators)
    ↓
Variance Detection
    - MISSING (expected but not observed)
    - UNEXPECTED (observed but not expected)
    - MODIFIED (attribute mismatch)
    - CONFLICT (identity collision)
    - DUPLICATE (name collision)
    - UNSUPPORTED (no baseline)
    ↓
Finding Generation (compliance integration)
    ↓
Persistent Comparison Record
    ↓
API-Accessible Results
    ↓
Historical Timeline
```
